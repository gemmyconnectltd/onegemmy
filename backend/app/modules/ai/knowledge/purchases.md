# Purchases and suppliers
The flow: a staff member raises a purchase request, a manager turns it into a purchase order, goods arrive and are received into stock, and receiving creates a supplier bill that is tracked until it is paid. A purchase order saved as Draft does nothing to stock or the accounts; receiving it adds the stock and records the amount you owe the supplier. Receiving cannot be undone from the order, and a received order can no longer be edited.

There is a supplier directory, purchase returns (tracked separately from sales returns) and spend-by-supplier reporting.
