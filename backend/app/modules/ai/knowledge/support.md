# Getting help and support
Pesaa AI (the "Ask Pesaa AI" button, or Ask Pesaa AI in the Support menu) is the fastest way to get help: it answers how-to questions from the Pesaa documentation and answers questions about your own data. For anything it cannot answer, or if you want to talk to a person, use the Support menu (the life-ring button, bottom right) to chat with the Pesaa team on WhatsApp, or email support@pesaa.io.

The Support menu also has "Take a tour", a short guided walkthrough of the main screens.
