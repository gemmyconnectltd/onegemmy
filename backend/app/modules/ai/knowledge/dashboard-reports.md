# Dashboard and reports
The Dashboard shows live numbers: sales, expenses, profit, cash, category breakdowns and low-stock warnings. The Reports area has Sales, Accounting, Inventory and Customer reports, each with date filters and detail views.

Reports show revenue by month, VAT collected, orders by status and top products by revenue, all computed from real transactions. Amounts are in your business's base currency. VAT is tracked automatically at the configured rate (18% for Rwanda).

To ask a question about your numbers (for example how much you sold last month), ask Pesaa AI; it uses the same data.
