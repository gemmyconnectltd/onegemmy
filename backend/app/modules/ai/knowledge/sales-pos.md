# Sales and point of sale
The Point of Sale screen works in any browser. Search or scan a product, add it to the cart and charge. Payment can be cash, mobile money or card. You can hold a sale and come back to it. Every sale reduces stock and posts to the accounts immediately, and prints a branded receipt with your logo.

Orders appear under Sales > Orders and become invoices automatically (see Accounting). A sale can be paid in full, partly, or on credit: for a credit or part-paid sale you must choose a customer and a due date. Returns are recorded under Sales > Returns; an approved return puts the goods back in stock and reverses the sale in the accounts.

A new order saved as Pending is a draft: it does not touch stock or the accounts until it is completed.
