# What is Pesaa
Pesaa is an all-in-one business management platform: sales and point of sale, inventory, accounting and invoicing, purchases and suppliers, customers (CRM), HR and payroll, manufacturing, reports and a mobile app. Everything lives in one place and every action updates everywhere else: a sale at the till reduces stock, records revenue and updates your accounts at the same moment.

It is for a single-location shop owner up to a multi-branch business with a back-office team. Every module is optional: turn on what you need today and add the rest later. Each business on Pesaa is completely isolated from every other business.

Pesaa is registered in Kigali, Rwanda. Pesaa AI is the built-in assistant: it answers questions from your own business data and from this help documentation.
