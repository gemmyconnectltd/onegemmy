# Customers and CRM
The Customers area holds your customers with their full purchase history, what they have paid and what they still owe. The CRM side has a visual deal pipeline from lead to closed sale, quotes, commissions, team targets and email campaigns.

To find customers who have not bought recently, ask Pesaa AI (for example "Which customers have not purchased in the last 60 days?"). Pesaa AI shows customer names and amounts only, not contact details.
