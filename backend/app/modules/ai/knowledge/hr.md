# HR and payroll
Employee directory (job title, department, hire date), attendance (clock-in tracking), leave requests with approval, payroll and recruiting (applicants through hiring stages). Departments start from six defaults or from an industry template (Retail, Manufacturing, Healthcare, Hospitality and more).

Employee and payroll data is sensitive: it is only visible to roles with HR permissions. Pesaa AI only reports headcounts and attendance/leave totals, never individual salaries, and never makes hiring, pay or disciplinary decisions.
