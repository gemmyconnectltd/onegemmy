# Accounting and invoicing
Every sale and purchase is posted to your books automatically. There is no manual reconciliation. Invoices come from your sales and carry your logo and brand colour; you can download a printable PDF.

Prices are VAT-inclusive: net = gross x 100/118 and VAT = gross x 18/118 at the Rwanda rate. Revenue and tax filings therefore agree.

Credit sales and partial payments: an invoice can be Unpaid, Partially Paid or Paid, and you record each payment against it. A payment can be reversed; the original entry is kept and an opposite entry is posted.

Expenses: add an expense (it starts as Pending), then Approve it. Only an approved expense is posted to the accounts. The accounting area also has the chart of accounts, journal entries and financial statements (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, trial balance), which can be exported.
