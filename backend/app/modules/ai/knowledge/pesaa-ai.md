# Pesaa AI assistant
Pesaa AI is a business copilot. Click "Ask Pesaa AI" in the top bar. It can: summarise sales, find top products, show low stock and reorder suggestions, show customer balances (receivables) and what you owe suppliers, summarise expenses, find inactive customers, give production and workforce summaries, and answer how-to questions from this documentation.

It can also summarise your Services (completed appointments and walk-ins per service, with revenue estimated from service prices) and draw simple bar charts alongside tables.

It can prepare drafts: a draft invoice (saved as a Pending order), a draft purchase order (saved as a Draft), or a payment-reminder email to a customer who owes you money. It always shows a preview first; nothing is saved or sent until you press Confirm, and a reminder cannot be sent to the same customer twice within 24 hours. It never changes posted accounting records, never changes prices or stock, and never invents numbers: every figure comes from your data. Reorder advice uses each supplier's lead time when you have recorded one (Inventory > Suppliers).

It only sees what your role and branch allow, and your monthly AI allowance depends on your plan.
