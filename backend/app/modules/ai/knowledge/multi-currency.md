# Currencies and exchange rates
Every business has a base currency (chosen at sign-up) in which its books, reports and VAT are kept. Settings > Financial Settings shows it. The base currency can only be changed while the business has recorded nothing; after the first transaction it is locked, because changing it would reinterpret every old amount in another currency.

To invoice, buy or spend in another currency, add an exchange rate in Settings (for example 1 USD = 1,450 RWF). A foreign-currency document keeps its original amount and also stores its value in your base currency at the rate on the day; later rate changes never alter past documents. Payments in another currency are converted at the payment-day rate and any difference is recorded as a foreign-exchange gain or loss. If no rate exists, Pesaa asks for one; it never assumes a rate.
