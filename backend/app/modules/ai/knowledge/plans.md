# Plans
Pesaa offers Free, Starter, Professional and Enterprise plans. Free: core dashboard, up to 2 users, core business modules, no credit card. Starter: up to 10 users, inventory and sales management. Professional: unlimited users, HR and accounting suite. Enterprise: multi-branch operations, custom integrations and priority support. There are no setup fees. For current prices or to change plan, contact Pesaa at info@pesaa.io.
