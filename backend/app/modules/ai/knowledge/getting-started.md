# Getting started
Create an account by choosing a business name and a country/currency. No credit card is needed for the Free plan. New accounts are approved by the Pesaa team before first login.

Your business starts with sensible defaults, including six standard departments (Sales, Finance, HR, Operations, Marketing, Customer Service) which you can rename or replace. Invite your team by adding employees with a role (for example Owner, Manager, Sales Staff, Inventory Staff); people only see what their role allows. You can start selling as soon as your first product is added. There is no separate go-live step.

Business details, brand colour, VAT and financial settings are in Settings.
