User Guide

Everything Pesaa does, in plain language

Pesaa is a single place to run sales, stock, books, purchasing, your team, and your customers — from one login. This guide walks through what's actually on screen, module by module, so you know where to go and what each part is for.

What you see may differ slightly depending on your plan — modules are switched on per business.

Getting started

Every Pesaa account starts with a short sign-up, followed by a quick review before you can sign in.

  • 1
    Create your account. On the sign-up page, step one asks for your name, phone number, email, and a password — your password is yours from the start; nobody sets it for you.
  • 2
    Tell us about your business. Step two covers your business name, type (sole proprietorship, partnership, LLC, or unregistered), industry, size, and location.
  • 3
    Wait for approval. New businesses are reviewed before they go live. You'll see a "pending approval" confirmation right away, and an email once your account is active.
  • 4
    Sign in. Once approved, log in with the email and password you chose at sign-up — you're taken straight to your dashboard.
💬
Multiple people at one businessAdditional staff are added by whoever manages your account, from inside Pesaa — they don't need to go through sign-up themselves.

Your dashboard /dashboard

The first screen you land on after signing in — a quick snapshot of the business before you head into a specific module. Everything on it links through to the fuller module pages covered below.

Sales /sales

The Sales menu covers two different jobs — worth knowing apart, since they're not the same screen.

  • →
    Sales Pipeline (the main Sales page) tracks deals as they move through stages — Leads, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Closed Won, Closed Lost. Each deal has a value, a probability, an expected close date, and notes. Use this for anything you're still negotiating: quotes, wholesale orders, ongoing conversations.
  • →
    Orders /sales/orders is where an actual sale gets rung up — pick a customer (or leave it as a walk-in), add products or product variants by searching name or SKU, adjust price, quantity, or discount per line, then mark the order Pending, Completed, or Cancelled. Completing an order posts it straight into your books.
⚠️
VAT is entered by handOrders show an 18% VAT column, but that figure is typed in per order rather than calculated automatically from a tax rate — double-check it before completing a sale.

Also on this menu: AnalyticsReturnsTargets

Inventory /inventory

Everything you stock, from a single item to a product with several variants.

  • →
    Add products one at a time or import a batch — each product has a name, SKU, category, brand, unit, price, cost, current stock, and a minimum-stock threshold, plus an optional photo.
  • →
    Variants let one product carry several versions — size, color, and so on — each tracked with its own stock and price, rolled up into the parent product's totals.
  • →
    Restock any low or out-of-stock item directly from its alert — record the quantity added, the reason, and any notes.

Also on this menu: CategoriesBrandsUnitsBatchesSerialsSuppliersTransfersWarranty

Accounting /accounting

Your books, kept in view: income, expenses, profit, and cash — updated automatically as sales come in, plus what you log by hand.

  • →
    A running view of total income, total expenses, net profit, margin, cash balance, and money owed to you, alongside a six-month income-vs-expenses chart.
  • →
    Log an expense with a title, amount, date, and category, or log income received outside of a normal sale — Pesaa records the underlying journal entry for you.
  • →
    Completed sales from the Orders screen post here automatically — you don't need to re-enter them.

Also on this menu: Chart of AccountsExpensesIncomeInvoicesReports

Procurement /procurement

What you buy from suppliers, and what your own staff have asked to order.

  • →
    A quick view of pending and draft purchase orders, your supplier count, total spend, and a spend-by-supplier breakdown.
  • →
    Purchase orders move through Draft → Approved → Received (or Cancelled), so you can see exactly what's on its way.
  • →
    Staff requests let your team ask for something to be ordered, for you to approve or decline.

Also on this menu: Purchase OrdersSuppliersRequestsReturns

HR /hr

Your team, in one list.

  • →
    Add an employee with their name, contact details, department, job title, hire date, salary, and employment status — Active, On Leave, or Terminated.
  • →
    Filter your team by status, and see totals for active staff and staff on leave at a glance.

Also on this menu: AttendanceLeavePayrollRecruiting

Customers /customers

Who buys from you, and what they've bought.

  • →
    Add a customer with contact details and a type — individual, business, VIP, or wholesale.
  • →
    Open any customer to see their full order history, total orders, and total amount spent.
  • →
    See your total customer count, how many are active, total revenue, and your top spender at a glance.

Also on this menu: AnalyticsLoyaltySegments

Manufacturing /manufacturing

For businesses that build or assemble what they sell.

  • →
    Work orders move through Draft → Scheduled → In Progress → Completed, each tied to a product, a quantity, and a scheduled date.
  • →
    See totals for work orders in progress, completed, and units produced overall.

Also on this menu: Work OrdersBill of MaterialsMaterialsAnalytics

Reports /reports

A read-only view across everything else — for when you need the numbers, not the data entry.

  • →
    Total revenue, VAT collected, refunds, and active customers, plus revenue by month and orders by status.
  • →
    A top products table ranked by revenue, drawn straight from your real orders.

Branding & appearance Settings → Appearance

The one screen in Settings that actually changes what your whole team sees.

  • →
    Theme — switch the whole account between light and dark.
  • →
    Brand color and logo — pick an accent color and upload your company logo (PNG, JPEG, or WEBP, up to 2MB); both apply for everyone at your business.
  • →
    Language — English, Ikinyarwanda, or Kiswahili.
  • →
    Currency — RWF, USD, EUR, KES, UGX, or TZS. This sets one currency for the whole account, not per sale.
🎨
Shared, not personalAppearance settings apply to your whole business, not just your own login — changing the logo or currency here changes it for every staff member.

Good to know

Why don't I see every module in this guide?

Modules are switched on per plan, and some screens are limited by your role. If something here isn't in your menu, it isn't part of your current plan.

Can different branches use different currencies?

No — currency is one setting for the whole account, chosen in Settings → Appearance.

Does VAT get calculated for me?

Not automatically. The Orders screen shows an 18% VAT column, but the amount is entered per order rather than computed from a stored tax rate.

What's the difference between Sales and Orders?

Sales is your pipeline of deals still in progress. Orders, under Sales, is where a completed sale actually gets recorded and posted to your books.
